We're Building a Greener Tomorrow

EcoWorld Earth Products was born in 2023 from a simple belief: sustainable choices should be easy, affordable, and accessible to everyone.

Our Story

Two Friends, One Mission

It started in 2023 in Bengaluru, when two best friends looked at the mountain of plastic packaging piling up around them and asked: "Why is doing the right thing so hard?"

Every shop, every delivery, every takeout order came wrapped in plastic that would outlast them by centuries. They knew better alternatives existed. They just weren't accessible or affordable.

So they built EcoWorld Earth Products — a company dedicated to making eco-friendly packaging the obvious choice for businesses across India. From biodegradable carry bags to compostable food containers, every product replaces something that would otherwise pollute our soil and oceans.

What began as a small operation shipping kraft bags from a Bengaluru apartment has grown into a full catalog of sustainable packaging solutions trusted by retailers, restaurants, and e-commerce brands.

Our Promise

Sustainability Commitment

We don't just sell eco products — we operate as an eco-first company end to end:

  • 100% Plastic-Free Packaging

    Every product we ship arrives in compostable or recyclable packaging. No bubble wrap, no poly mailers, no exceptions.

  • Carbon-Neutral Shipping

    We offset the carbon footprint of every delivery through verified reforestation and renewable energy credits.

  • Certified Biodegradable Materials

    All products meet BIS and international compostability standards. They break down in 90-180 days in composting conditions.

  • Responsible Sourcing

    We work exclusively with manufacturers who use FSC-certified paper, recycled content, and non-toxic inks and adhesives.

  • Minimal Digital Footprint

    We keep our digital operations lean — one focused social channel, optimized web assets — because even digital waste has a carbon cost.
